What is TMJ dysfunction?+

The temporomandibular joint (TMJ) connects your jaw to your skull. When the joint and surrounding muscles are strained, you may feel jaw pain, clicking, tightness, headaches or a jaw that won’t fully open.

How does Emface treat TMJ?+

Emface combines synchronized radiofrequency (RF) with HIFES, or high-intensity facial electromagnetic stimulation. RF gently warms the tissue, eases muscle spasm and boosts local blood flow. HIFES activates the jaw muscles to relieve TMJ dysfunction and support range of motion.

Do night guards help TMJ?+

For many patients, yes. A custom night guard, also called an occlusal splint, protects your teeth from grinding and eases strain on your jaw joint and muscles. A guard does not stop grinding itself, so if your muscles stay tight, Dr. Adrianna can pair it with Emface or Botox.

How is Emface different from Botox for TMJ?+

Botox uses injections to temporarily weaken the jaw muscle, and it wears off, so it needs repeating every few months. Emface uses no needles and works to retrain and rebalance the muscles behind your pain.

Can Emface be combined with other TMJ treatments?+

Yes. Dr. Adrianna can combine Emface with a custom night guard or Botox, and keep monitoring your bite and teeth over time.

Is Emface FDA-cleared for TMJ?+

Yes. Emface is FDA-cleared to treat TMJ dysfunction and associated pain. It is also cleared to relieve muscle spasm, re-educate muscles, increase local blood flow and maintain or improve jaw range of motion.

How many sessions will I need?+

Most patients have four 20-minute sessions, one week apart. Dr. Adrianna tailors the plan to you at your exam.

Does it hurt?+

No. Most patients feel gentle, rhythmic contractions and a warming sensation. It should never feel painful or burning.

When will I feel relief?+

Some patients notice relief after the first session, and most notice a change once the series is complete. Results vary.
